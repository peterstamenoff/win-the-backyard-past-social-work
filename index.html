Sam’s Club | Win the Backyard Campaign

Win the Backyard was a seasonal Sam’s Club campaign designed to inspire members to refresh their outdoor spaces while showcasing outdoor furniture and seasonal products.

I was the designer for the social media portion, working closely with a copywriter and art director. My focus was on creating engaging visuals with clear messaging, strong visual hierarchy, and a consistent brand experience.
